Subject: L&T Construction Awarded Mega Contract for Eight-Lane Expressway Project

Mumbai, October 14, 2024: The construction arm of Larsen & Toubro has secured a 'Significant' order from the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) for the development of an eight-lane access-controlled expressway in the state of Maharashtra.

The project scope includes the construction of a 42km stretch from Section KM 124 to KM 166. The contract involves significant structural work including 4 major bridges, 12 minor bridges, and 2 interchanges. The total project value is estimated at ₹2,150 Crore.

The project is part of the Bharatmala Pariyojana Phase-I and is expected to be executed within a period of 36 months using the EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) model.

Commenting on this win, Mr. S.N. Subrahmanyan, CEO & Managing Director, L&T, said, "This award reinforces our leadership in the infrastructure space and our commitment to building the nation's critical transport corridors."
