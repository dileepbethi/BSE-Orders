# Vision

OrderIQ is an Enterprise Procurement Intelligence Platform that transforms unstructured corporate order announcements into structured, searchable, and actionable business intelligence.

The platform continuously collects corporate order announcements from Indian stock exchanges, downloads official disclosure documents, extracts key procurement information using AI, validates the extracted data, and provides enterprise-grade analytics for decision makers.

The long-term vision is to become the most trusted procurement intelligence platform for investors, enterprises, consultants, research firms, government organizations, and financial institutions.

---

# Mission

Reduce the time required to analyze procurement announcements from hours to minutes by combining automation, AI extraction, searchable historical records, and professional review workflows.

---

# Product Philosophy

OrderIQ is not a CRUD application.

OrderIQ is not an ERP.

OrderIQ is not a stock market app.

OrderIQ is a Procurement Intelligence Platform.

Every feature must help users answer one or more of the following questions:

• Which company won a new order?
• Who awarded the order?
• What is the order value?
• When was it announced?
• Is the AI extraction correct?
• What trends can be identified?
• Which companies are improving?
• Which sectors are growing?

If a feature does not improve these workflows, it should not be prioritized.
